# TELPSAM Portal — Student Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test the TELPSAM Alumni & Mentorship Portal. You'll play the
part of a **student**. Please go through the steps below and jot down anything
that's broken, confusing, slow, or just looks off. There are no wrong answers —
we want your honest reactions.

**Before you start**
- Use a **real email address you can open** (you'll get a confirmation email).
- It helps to test on a **phone** if you can, since most students will.
- Some steps need a coordinator to act (approving you, matching you). If nothing
  happens, that's your cue to tell the coordinator you're waiting.

**The link:** _[your coordinator will paste the site link here]_

---

## Part 1 — Create your account
1. Open the link. On the home page, tap **Join**, then **I'm a student**.
2. Notice the note that says a coordinator reviews every student. Enter your
   name, email, and a password (at least 8 characters). Tap **Create account**.
3. Check your email for a confirmation link and click it. (Check spam too.)
4. Come back and **sign in**.

**Tell us:** Was anything confusing? Did the confirmation email arrive quickly?

## Part 2 — Complete your profile
5. You should be asked to complete your profile. Fill in:
   - Gender, and your **school / institution**.
   - **Level of education** (SHS 3 / Completed SHS / Tertiary). Watch the
     **Class/Level** dropdown — its options should change to match your level.
   - **Church branch** — try searching the list; then try the **Other** option
     and type a branch that isn't listed.
   - **Your phone number** — change the country in the dropdown and check the
     number field accepts the right length.
   - **Parent/guardian name and phone.**
6. Submit. You should land on an **"awaiting approval"** screen.

**Tell us:** Did every required field make sense? Did the phone and branch
pickers behave? Anything hard to tap on a phone?

## Part 3 — After you're approved
> A coordinator now approves your account. Wait for the go-ahead, then sign in.

7. Open the **Alumni Directory**. Browse the cards. Try the **search box** and
   the **industry / interest filters**. Try "Clear filters".
8. Open an alumnus profile. Read it. Tap **Request mentorship**, choose a type,
   and write a short message. Send it. Check **My Requests** shows it.

**Tell us:** Was the directory easy to browse? Did search/filters work?

## Part 4 — Once you're matched
> A coordinator matches you with a mentor. Wait for it, then open **My
> Mentorship**.

9. Open the conversation. Read the guidance/starters box at the top. Say hello.
10. Send a normal message and confirm it appears.
11. Now test the safety net: send a message that includes a **phone number**
    (e.g. "call me on 024 123 4567") and another like "let's **meet up**". The
    message should still send — you won't see anything special, and that's fine.
12. Open **Request a phone call** and send a request.
13. If a **check-in** prompt appears ("How is it going?"), answer it. Try the
    "I have a concern" option once to see what happens.
14. Notice the note that the mentorship runs for **3 months**.

**Tell us:** Did messaging feel smooth? Were the extra options (call request,
check-in) clear?

## Part 5 — Reach the coordinators
15. In the menu, open **Contact Coordinators**. Send a question. (A coordinator
    can reply; you'll see the reply as "TELPSAM Program Coordinators".)

## Part 6 — Poke around
16. Tap the **TELPSAM logo** — it should take you home without logging you out.
17. On a phone, open the **menu (hamburger)** and check every link opens.
18. Open **My Profile** and try editing something, then save.

---

## What to report
For anything you noticed, note: **what page**, **what you did**, **what you
expected**, **what happened**. Screenshots help a lot, especially on mobile.
Please flag:
- Anything broken or that showed an error.
- Copy that was confusing, had a typo, or felt off.
- Anything that looked squashed or hard to use on your phone.
- Steps that took too long or where you got stuck.

Thank you! 🙏
