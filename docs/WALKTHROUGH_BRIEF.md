# TELPSAM Mentorship Portal — Walkthrough Video Brief

**Goal:** a 2–3 minute screen-recorded walkthrough showing how the portal works and how it keeps students safe.
**URL:** https://mentorship.telpsam.com
**Length:** aim for 2:30 (hard cap 3:00).

---

## Before you record (5-minute prep)

- **Use TEST accounts only — never a real student's or minor's details.** The site is in test mode; accounts and data may be reset.
- Have three test accounts ready and signed in on separate browsers/profiles:
  1. a **Coordinator** (admin),
  2. an **Alumnus** (mentor) with a completed, approved profile,
  3. a **Student** (mentee) with a completed profile.
- Pre-create one **matched mentorship** with a few friendly messages already in it, so the chat isn't empty on camera.
- Close unrelated tabs/notifications. Record at 1080p. Keep the cursor calm and slow.
- Don't show real phone numbers or emails on screen — blur or use obvious dummies.

---

## The script (narration + what to show)

**1. Opening — what it is (0:00–0:20)**
> "This is the TELPSAM mentorship portal — a safe, coordinated space where students are mentored by alumni who have gone ahead of them. Every conversation is private and protected by the Program Coordinators."

Show: the landing/login page, then the logged-in dashboard.

**2. Joining (0:20–0:45)**
> "Anyone joins by creating an account as a student or an alumnus and completing a short profile. A coordinator reviews and approves every account before it becomes active."

Show: the Join page (student vs alumnus), a completed profile, then the "pending approval" state.

**3. Coordinators approve and match (0:45–1:15)**
> "On the coordinator side, pending students and alumni appear for review. Once approved, a coordinator matches a student with a suitable mentor."

Show: the admin Students/Alumni pages approving an account, then matching a student to a mentor.

**4. The mentorship conversation (1:15–1:55)**
> "Matched pairs get a private one-to-one chat, with conversation starters to help them begin. Messages appear instantly, with read receipts. A clear reminder sits at the top: keep everything in the portal — no phone numbers, money, or meet-ups. If a message breaks those rules, the portal automatically flags it for the coordinators to review."

Show: the chat — send a message, point to the Rules-of-Engagement banner and read receipt. (Optional: type a message with a phone number to show it getting flagged.)

**5. Safety and support (1:55–2:20)**
> "Members can do a quick private check-in on how the mentorship is going, request a phone call that a coordinator facilitates, or report a concern at any time. Crucially, coordinators only ever see the flagged message — never the whole private conversation."

Show: the check-in buttons, the "request a call" and "report a concern" options; then the coordinator's Alerts page showing a flagged item.

**6. Time-bound and close (2:20–2:40)**
> "Each mentorship runs for three months with a one-time extension, then ends automatically. It's simple, guided, and built to keep our students safe."

Show: the "runs until…" note on a mentorship, then end on the dashboard or logo.

---

## Tips

- **Lead with safety.** If anything gets cut for time, keep sections 4 and 5 — the private-and-protected story is the point.
- Narrate in plain, warm language; avoid jargon like "RLS" or "server action."
- One take per section is fine; stitch them together. Add soft background music low in the mix.
- End with the URL on screen: **mentorship.telpsam.com**.
