# TELPSAM Portal — Alumni Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test the TELPSAM Alumni & Mentorship Portal. You'll play the
part of an **alumnus/alumna** (a past TELPSAM member willing to mentor a
student). Please work through the steps and note anything broken, confusing,
slow, or off. Honest reactions are exactly what we need.

**Before you start**
- Use a **real email address you can open** (you'll get a confirmation email).
- Try it on both a **computer and a phone** if you can.
- Some steps need a coordinator to act (approving/publishing you, matching you
  with a student). If nothing happens, tell the coordinator you're waiting.

**The link:** _[your coordinator will paste the site link here]_

---

## Part 1 — Create your account
1. Open the link. Tap **Join**, then **I'm an alumnus**.
2. Enter your name, email, and a password (8+ characters). Create the account.
3. Click the confirmation link in your email (check spam too), then **sign in**.
4. You'll be asked to **agree to the Rules of Engagement**. Skim them, tick the
   box, and continue.

**Tell us:** Anything confusing? Did the confirmation email arrive quickly?

## Part 2 — Build your alumni profile
5. Open **My Profile** and fill everything in:
   - Title, gender, campus/fellowship, graduation year, current role,
     organization, industry, qualifications.
   - **Phone number** — note it says it's private (coordinators only). Try the
     country dropdown.
   - **Church branch** — try the list and the **Other** free-text option.
   - **Interests** (this one is optional).
   - **Short bio** — watch the **word counter**. Try saving with only a few
     words (it should stop you), then write between **30 and 150 words**.
   - **Upload a photo** if you can.
   - Tick **"Make my profile visible in the student directory."**
6. Save. You should see an **"awaiting review"** status.

**Tell us:** Did the bio word limit and the phone/branch pickers behave? Did the
photo upload work? Any field unclear?

## Part 3 — After you're approved
> A coordinator approves and publishes your profile. Wait for the go-ahead.

7. Ask the coordinator (or a student tester) to confirm you now appear in the
   **Alumni Directory** with your photo, role, and interests.

**Tell us:** Does your directory card look right?

## Part 4 — Once you're matched with a mentee
> A coordinator assigns you a student. Open **My Mentees / My Mentorship**.

8. Open the conversation. Read the **starters / guidance** box at the top. Say
   hello to your mentee.
9. Send a normal message and confirm it appears.
10. Test the safety net: send a message containing a **phone number** and one
    that suggests **meeting up**. It should still send — nothing visible happens,
    which is expected.
11. Try **Request a phone call**.
12. Notice the mentorship runs for **3 months**. If you're near the end (or want
    to see it), try **Request a one-time 2-week extension** — it should say it
    was sent to the coordinators, and you shouldn't be able to request twice.
13. Try **End this mentorship** on a test pairing to see the read-only state.
    (Use a throwaway pairing — ending can't be undone by you.)

**Tell us:** Did messaging and the extra actions feel clear and trustworthy?

## Part 5 — Reach the coordinators
14. In the menu, open **Contact Coordinators** and send a question. A reply will
    appear as "TELPSAM Program Coordinators".

## Part 6 — Poke around
15. Tap the **TELPSAM logo** — it should go home without logging you out.
16. On a phone, open the **menu (hamburger)** and check every link works.
17. Re-open **My Profile**, edit something, and save again.

---

## What to report
For anything you noticed, note: **what page**, **what you did**, **what you
expected**, **what happened**. Screenshots help, especially on mobile. Please
flag:
- Anything broken or that showed an error.
- Confusing copy, typos, or wording that felt off for an older/professional audience.
- Anything squashed or awkward on a phone.
- Places you got stuck or that felt slow.

We especially want to know: **does the portal feel safe, respectful, and
official enough** for alumni to trust it? Thank you! 🙏
